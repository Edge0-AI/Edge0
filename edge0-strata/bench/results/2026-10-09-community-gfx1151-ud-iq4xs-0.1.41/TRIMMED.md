# Trimmed files

Raw dumps, logs and telemetry were left out of this report to keep the repository small. The README, summaries,
`analysis/`, the server configurations and the long-sweep engine logs are unchanged. The files below exist on the
reporter's machine, not in this folder.

| file or directory | why |
|---|---|
| per-request responses and grader output (`quality/*.jsonl`) | raw dump; the four failed tasks are in `analysis/failures.json` |
| `needle-requests/`, `needle-native-requests/` | raw requests and responses |
| `parallel-qfuse*-borrow*/` | raw requests and SSE chunks |
| `*-engine.log` other than the two long-sweep arms, `*-server.log`, `*-health.json`, per-stage `<label>.log` | logs |
| `telemetry.jsonl` and the hwmon samples | raw samples; extremes are in `analysis/summary.json` and `analysis/thermal-summary.json` |
| per-tensor manifests: tensor types, conversions, compat-bf16, MTP files | regenerated by the commands in `REPRODUCE.md` from the pinned revisions |
